1. Introduction

At Iyal Surveys, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Iyal Couch Global Ventures And Business Solutions, the operator of Iyal Surveys, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you visit or use our platform at iyalsurveys.online.

This includes information collected through account registration, dashboard activity, reward processing, security checks, and third-party survey services.

By creating an account or using Iyal Surveys, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires specific consent for certain data processing activities, we will request that consent separately.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Iyal Surveys, we may collect the following categories of information:

Category What When
Account Name, username, email address and country. During registration and account updates.
Profile Gender, date of birth, avatar information and phone / WhatsApp number. When you complete or update your profile.
Verification Identity verification status, such as verified, pending or failed, and related verification information where required. When account verification is performed.
Activity Survey participation, completed surveys, dashboard visits, active participation, referrals, balance, earnings and reward records. When you use the platform.
Withdrawal Airtime phone number or bank details, depending on your selected withdrawal method. When you request a withdrawal.
Device & Security IP address, browser and device information, operating system, connection information, login activity, and device identifiers or fingerprint signals used for security and fraud prevention. When you visit, sign in or use the dashboard.
Support Messages, complaints, screenshots and other information you voluntarily provide when contacting support. When you contact us.

Important: We do not ask for your bank PIN, card CVV or account password through support messages. Your password is handled through the authentication system rather than stored as readable text by us. Never share sensitive security credentials with anyone claiming to represent Iyal Surveys.

Device identifiers and fingerprint signals may help us recognise suspicious activity or repeated use of devices. These signals are not guaranteed to identify a person and are not, by themselves, proof of fraud.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for legitimate platform operations, security, reward processing and support, including:

  • To create, maintain and manage user accounts.
  • To provide access to surveys and other available opportunities.
  • To process, record and verify earnings and withdrawals.
  • To track survey completions and eligible platform activity.
  • To manage referrals, rewards and leaderboard participation.
  • To verify age eligibility and account ownership where required.
  • To detect and investigate suspicious activity, duplicate accounts and possible fraud.
  • To protect the platform, users and our third-party service relationships.
  • To respond to support requests and account complaints.
  • To comply with applicable laws and legitimate legal requests.
  • To send important account, security, payout and service notices.

4. Activity & Rewards

Iyal Surveys records certain platform activities to operate its reward system. This may include eligible survey completions, dashboard visits, active participation, referrals and other activities defined by the platform.

Background or inactive time is not necessarily counted as active participation. Activity records may be used to calculate eligibility for applicable rewards, referrals, streaks or leaderboard programmes.

Survey rewards are also subject to confirmation from the relevant survey provider. A reward may be delayed, rejected or reversed if the provider reports that a completion was invalid, duplicated, incomplete or otherwise ineligible.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Iyal Surveys and its service providers may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to support essential website functions, maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve performance, protect accounts and track eligible activity.

Our third-party survey providers may also use their own cookies or tracking technologies when you choose to access their surveys or offerwalls. Their collection and use of information are governed by their own privacy policies and settings.

Essential technologies may be necessary for the website to function properly. Where non-essential cookies or tracking technologies require consent under applicable law, we will seek consent through an appropriate mechanism. You can also manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may affect some website functions.

6. Third-Party Survey Providers & Services

Some survey opportunities on Iyal Surveys are supplied by independent third-party networks. CPX Research is currently available on our platform.

We are working towards introducing BitLabs, while Wannads and other providers may be considered for future integration. These services should not be considered active on Iyal Surveys until they are officially integrated and made available to users.

When you choose to access a third-party survey provider, certain information may be shared with that provider to support survey access, account identification, eligibility, participation tracking and reward attribution. This may include a platform user identifier and other information required by the particular integration.

The third-party provider may independently collect additional information from you, including survey answers, demographic information, technical information and other details under its own privacy practices. Please review the provider's privacy policy before participating.

We aim to share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant integration and platform purpose. The exact information shared may differ between providers and may change when integrations are updated.

  • CPX Research Terms & Conditions
  • BitLabs Privacy Policy (for reference if and when the service becomes available).

Third-party providers operate independently of Iyal Surveys and may process information under their own policies. We do not control all aspects of their data processing. However, this does not remove our responsibility for handling information within our own platform appropriately.

7. Withdrawals — Bank & Airtime

When you request a withdrawal, we collect the information needed to process your selected payout method:

  • Airtime withdrawal: the phone number that should receive the airtime.
  • Bank withdrawal: bank name, account number and account name, as required for the transfer.

We use this information to process your withdrawal, maintain transaction records, investigate payout problems and help prevent fraudulent transactions.

Withdrawal records may be retained for accounting, security, dispute resolution and legal purposes. We do not use bank or airtime details for unrelated marketing purposes.

Protect yourself: Iyal Surveys will never ask you to send money to unlock a withdrawal. We will never ask for your bank PIN or card CVV. If anyone asks you to pay to withdraw, report it through Support.

8. Data Security — Important Acknowledgement

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational security measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. These measures may include access restrictions, secure connections and account monitoring.

However, no website, application, internet transmission or electronic storage system is 100% secure. Despite reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security or eliminate every risk associated with internet use, including unauthorised access, system failure or incidents involving third-party services.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential, using a secure device where possible and reporting suspicious account activity.

We do not publicly disclose sensitive internal security and fraud-prevention procedures where doing so could make it easier for individuals to bypass them. If your account access is restricted, you may contact Support and request a review.

If we become aware of a personal data breach that requires notification under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with our legal obligations.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority where applicable.

You may update certain profile details through your account settings. Some information, such as verification-related details or financial transaction records, may be restricted from editing or deletion where necessary for security, legal, accounting or operational reasons.

To exercise a privacy right or ask a question about your information, contact us at support@iyalsurveys.online or through our Support page.

We may need to verify your account ownership before fulfilling certain requests. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

10. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including operating your account, processing rewards, maintaining transaction records, preventing fraud, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods. For example:

  • Account and profile information may be retained while your account remains active.
  • Survey, reward and activity records may be retained to verify earnings and resolve provider disputes.
  • Withdrawal and transaction records may be retained for accounting, security and legal requirements.
  • Security and fraud-prevention records may be retained for a reasonable period to investigate suspicious activity and protect the platform.
  • Support communications may be retained for as long as needed to resolve issues and maintain relevant records.

When information is no longer reasonably required and there is no legal or legitimate reason to retain it, we will take appropriate steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our platform, services, integrations or legal obligations change.

When we make changes, we will update the date displayed at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may also provide a notice through the platform or another appropriate communication channel.

Where applicable law requires renewed consent for a change, we will request that consent. Continued use of the platform does not replace any consent that must be obtained separately under applicable law.
