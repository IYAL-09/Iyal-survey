1. Introduction

At Iyal Surveys, your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform, including surveys from third-party partners. By creating an account or using Iyal Surveys, you agree to this Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain the platform
  • To process earnings and withdrawals
  • To deliver surveys from third-party partners
  • To track survey completions and active participation for rewards
  • To verify you are 18+ and the real owner of the account
  • To prevent fraud and abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To send account, payout and support notices

4. Activity & Rewards

Rewards are based on real, active participation — for example, completing surveys, visiting your dashboard, and actively engaging while surveys are open. Background or inactive time is not counted as activity. This keeps the reward pool fair for genuine users and protects it from abuse.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience and track survey participation. Third-party survey providers such as CPX Research, BitLabs, and Wannads may also use cookies or tracking technologies.

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform or manage cookies via your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Surveys

Some surveys on Iyal Surveys are provided by third-party partners, including CPX Research, BitLabs (Prodege), and Wannads. When you choose a provider, we share only the minimum identifier needed for that provider to show you surveys and credit your completion. These partners may collect additional information according to their own privacy policies.

  • CPX Privacy Policy
  • CPX Terms & Conditions
  • BitLabs (Prodege) Privacy Policy
  • BitLabs (Prodege) Terms & Conditions

By participating in these surveys, you agree to the privacy practices of these third-party providers. We are not responsible for how these third parties handle your information after you accept their consent.

7. Withdrawals — Bank & Airtime

When you request a withdrawal, we ask for one of these, depending on what you choose:

  • Airtime withdrawal: phone number to receive airtime.
  • Bank withdrawal: bank name, account number and account name.

We use this information only to send your reward and to keep a payout record. We never use it for marketing, and we never ask for your bank PIN or card CVV.

Protect yourself: Iyal Surveys will never ask you to send money to unlock a withdrawal. If anyone asks you to pay to withdraw, report it in Support.

8. Data Security — Important Acknowledgement

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational security measures — including access controls, encrypted connections, and ongoing monitoring — to safeguard your information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no website, application, internet transmission, or electronic storage system is 100% secure. Despite our best efforts, we cannot guarantee absolute security, and we cannot eliminate all risks associated with using the internet, including hacking, interception, unauthorised access, system failure, or third-party breaches beyond our control.

By registering for and using Iyal Surveys, you acknowledge and accept this risk. You understand that you provide your information voluntarily, that you are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential, and that you agree to use the platform on this basis. If you do not agree, please do not register or use the platform.

We intentionally do not publish the details of our internal security and fraud-prevention checks, as doing so would help fraudsters bypass them. If your access is restricted, you may contact Support for review.

You also have a role to play: keep your password private, log out on shared devices, and report any suspicious activity to Support immediately.

9. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and operational requirements. You can update your name, username, phone, gender, avatar and date of birth (if not locked after verification) in Profile. Withdrawal and payout records may be kept as required by law even after account closure.

Contact us at iyaldevelopers@gmail.com or via Support. We may ask you to verify your identity before sharing account data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after a change indicates acceptance of the updated Policy. We will update the date at the top and, for important changes, notify you in-app.
