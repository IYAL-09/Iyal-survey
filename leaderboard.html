How the leaderboard works

Clear rules. Fair rankings. No hidden scoring.

1. Qualify with four surveys

Complete at least four successful surveys between Monday and Sunday, and keep all required profile details complete.

2. How positions are decided

More successful surveys rank first. If survey counts match, higher survey earnings rank next. Active time and dashboard visits are only tie-breakers, not extra points.

3. Active time

This is the time the app records while you're actively using it. It helps separate users who are otherwise tied. Leaving the app open while inactive does not mean unlimited active time.

4. Dashboard visits

This shows how many qualifying visits to your Dashboard were recorded this week. Zero visits are shown as 0; visits are only a later tie-breaker.

5. Weekly prizes

1st: ₦1,000 · 2nd: ₦500 · 3rd: ₦300 · 4th: ₦100 · 5th: ₦100. Positions 6–10 receive a leaderboard badge, not a cash prize.

6. Weekly reset and payment

The competition runs Monday to Sunday, Nigeria time. Results are allowed a 24-hour waiting period, then the previous week's results are finalised and prizes are credited automatically after finalisation.

Important: “Survey earnings” are the earnings recorded from successful surveys during this week. “Leaderboard prize” is a separate competition reward. A displayed current prize is an estimate based on your live position; final prizes are determined when the week is finalised. Activity time and visits do not replace the four-survey requirement.
