Who We Are

Iyal Surveys is an online rewards platform operated by Iyal Couch Global Ventures And Business Solutions, a business registered in Nigeria.

We connect users with trusted third-party survey and offer providers. Our goal is to give you a simple and transparent way to earn rewards by participating in surveys and completing approved tasks — without confusion, without hidden tricks.

What We Do

We partner with reputable advertising and survey networks to display available surveys and offers to our users. When you complete a survey successfully, we receive a reward from the survey provider, and a portion of that reward is credited to your account balance.

We bring surveys to you

You don't need to search the internet for surveys. We bring CPX Research, BitLabs and Wannads together in one dashboard.

We track & credit fairly

Your balance, total earned, surveys completed and referrals are shown clearly in your dashboard and profile.

How It Works

1

Referrals

You can also invite friends with your referral link. When your referrals participate genuinely, you earn referral rewards as shown in your Referrals page. Fake referrals, multiple accounts, or manipulated activity are not counted.

Important Information

Survey availability, eligibility, and completion status are determined entirely by third-party survey providers. Iyal Surveys does not control survey qualification criteria or disqualifications.

A reward may be reversed if the provider later determines the completion was invalid, duplicated, incomplete, or ineligible. Only valid and confirmed rewards can be withdrawn.

No payment to withdraw: We will never ask you to send money to unlock a withdrawal, and we will never ask for your bank PIN or card CVV. If anyone asks you to pay, report it in Support.
