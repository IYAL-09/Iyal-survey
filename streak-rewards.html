How Streak Rewards Work

Understand how to earn, claim and receive your rewards.

1. Qualifying survey rewards

  • A completed survey qualifies when your credited share is at least ₦100.
  • You must manually claim each qualifying survey.
  • Each qualifying survey can be claimed only once.
  • Claims must be made before midnight on the same Nigeria calendar day the survey was completed.
  • Unclaimed survey rewards expire at midnight. Your normal survey earnings are not affected.

2. Weekly challenge

  • Each challenge runs from Monday to Sunday, Nigeria time.
  • You may take up to two rest days.
  • You need at least five distinct active days to qualify for a weekly payout.
  • You must also have at least one qualifying survey claim during that week.
  • Completing zero to four active days means no weekly payout.
  • Several claims on one day still count as only one active day.

3. Points and weekly bonus

  • Survey claims earn base points equal to 10% of your credited survey amount.
  • For example, a ₦500 credited survey share earns 50 base points.
  • If you meet the weekly requirements, you receive an additional 10% bonus on your week's base points.
  • One point is equal to ₦1.
  • Weekly rewards are settled into your wallet every Monday at midnight Nigeria time.

4. Daily visit reward

  • Complete 10 qualifying Dashboard visits to unlock 10 points for that day.
  • Qualifying visits must be separated by at least 30 minutes.
  • You must manually claim the visit reward after reaching 10 visits.
  • Only one visit reward can be claimed per day.
  • Unclaimed visit rewards expire at midnight.
  • Visit rewards count as one active day, but visits alone cannot satisfy the weekly survey requirement.

5. Reversed surveys and point recovery

  • If a survey is reversed before weekly settlement, its pending Streak reward is removed.
  • If a survey is reversed after its Streak reward has been paid, the paid Streak amount is recorded as a debt.
  • Future manually claimed Streak rewards are used to recover that debt.
  • Your normal survey earnings and regular wallet balance are separate from Streak debt.
  • Your current point balance may become negative while a debt remains.
  • Claimed points can recover debt even in a week when you do not meet the five-day payout requirement.
  • If you do not qualify for weekly payout, any remaining positive points for that week are forfeited after applicable debt recovery.

6. Records and retention

Streak reward records are normally retained for six months. Records connected to an outstanding debt are preserved until that debt has been fully recovered.

All claims and settlements are verified by Iyal Surveys' secure system. Displayed weekly payout figures are estimates until settlement is completed.
